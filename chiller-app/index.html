Approach temperature

Evaporator approach = leaving chilled-water temperature − saturated evaporating temperature.

Condenser approach = saturated condensing temperature − leaving condenser-water temperature.

A rising approach points to worsening heat transfer, but flow, sensors, refrigerant distribution, oil logging and load must be checked before condemning tubes.

Safety

Follow the exact manufacturer IOM/service manual and site LOTO procedures. Never bypass a safety to keep a chiller running. Do not alter service-level parameters unless the exact machine procedure is confirmed.
