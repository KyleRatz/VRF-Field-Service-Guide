ARTICLE I. NAME AND LOCATION
Section 1
The name of the Nonprofit Corporation is South Montgomery Girls Softball League, Inc.
(herein referred to as "SMGSL" or the "League"). The principal office shall be located in
Montgomery County, Texas. The League may have such other office as the Board of Directors
(also referenced herein as the “Board”) shall determine.
Section 2
SMGSL is affiliated with and operates under the current year's rules and guidelines of USA
Softball, except where League rules have been adopted and shall supersede USA rules. USA
structure is as follows:
USA----Texas Region----Texas State----District XIV-----SMGSL
ARTICLE II. PURPOSE
Section 1
The purpose of SMGSL is to provide an organized and supervised competitive program of
softball in the local area. In so doing, the primary objective of the League is to promote and
develop an environment which emphasizes fair competition, good sportsmanship, and fun.
The attainment of exceptional skills or winning games shall be secondary.
Girls and young women from the age of three (3) through eighteen (18) are eligible to
participate in the League. A player’s age for eligibility and Division placement is based on
their age as of September 1st of the current playing season. The age eligibility requirements
for each Division are as follows:
A. The Blast Ball Division will consist of girls 3 through 4 years of age.
B. The 6 and Under Division will consist of girls 5 through 6 years of age.
C. The 8 and Under Division will consist of girls 7 through 8 years of age.
D. The 10 and Under Division will consist of girls 9 through 10 years of age.
E. The 12 and Under Division will consist of girls 11 through 12 years of age.
F. The Mixed Division will consist of girls 13 through 18 years of age.

Section 2
The League shall not engage in political lobbying or otherwise attempt to influence
legislation. The League shall not participate in any political campaign on behalf of or in
opposition to any candidate for public office. The League shall comply with all laws
pertaining to (a) a corporation exempt from federal income tax under Section 501(c)(3) of the
Tax Code and (b) a corporation, contributions to which are deductible under Section 17(c) (2)
of the Code.
ARTICLE III. DIRECTORS
Section 1. Duties
The regulation and management of the affairs of the League, including the control and
disposition of its property and funds, shall be vested in a Board of Directors. The Secretary
shall be responsible for maintaining written records of job duties and activities. It shall be the
duty of the Board of Directors to manage all activities related to SMGSL for proper operation,
safety, and security during all scheduled League games. The Board of Directors shall promote
communication with General Members on SMGSL activities.
Section 2. Board Size
The size of the Board shall be determined by the Executive Committee with the approval of
the Board, but in no case shall the size of the Board be less than seven (7) members. Any
decrease in the size of the Board shall not have the effect of shortening the term of office of
any incumbent Director.
Section 3. Terms
All Board Members shall be elected to a one (1) year term. All one (1) year terms shall expire
on June 30th of each year.
Section 4. Removal from Office
Any member of the Board of Directors may be removed from office and have their
membership in the League revoked by a vote of not less than two-thirds (2/3rds) of the
members of the Board of Directors, with no vote being placed by the member who is
being considered for removal. Said vote must be conducted during a regular or special
meeting of the members of the Board of Directors. Written notice of at least seventy-two
(72) hours must be given to the Board member who is being considered for removal.
A. Any Board member who fails to attend three consecutive regular meetings without a written
or oral excuse may be terminated as a Board member, as provided above.

Section 5. Reinstatement
Upon written request signed by a former member of the Board of Directors and filed with the 
Secretary, the Board of Directors may, by the affirmative vote of two-thirds (2/3rds) of the members
of the Board of Directors, reinstate such former member of the Board of Directors to his or her 
formerly occupied seat on the Board of Directors upon such terms as the Board of Directors deem 
appropriate. It is understood that a reinstatement will not be considered if the vacant position has 
been filled by the Board prior to the Board’s receipt of the written request.
Section 6. Board Vacancies
Any vacancy occurring on the Board of Directors, whether by resignation, removal, death, or
by increase in the number of Directors, may be filled by the affirmative vote of the majority
of the remaining Directors, though less than a quorum, of the Board of Directors. A Director
elected to fill a vacancy shall be elected for the unexpired term of his or her predecessor in
office, or in the instance a vacancy is due to the increase in the number of Directors, the
Director’s term shall expire at the end of the then existing term for the previously elected
Directors.
Section 7. Meetings of Directors
Regular meetings of the Board shall be scheduled and held monthly. The Board shall hold
meetings at least once a month between July 1st and June 30th of each fiscal year. No audio
or video recordings of regular, special, or committee meetings are allowed for purposes of
documenting minutes or otherwise. In the event such recordings are made by any member,
said member may be relieved of their duties, if any, following the applicable procedure set
forth in these by-laws.
Section 8. Special Meetings
Special meetings of the Board of Directors may be called by the President and shall be called
by the Secretary on the written request of two (2) Directors. Notice of each special meeting
of the Board of Directors shall be given to each Director at least two (2) days before the date
of the meeting.
Section 9. Quorum
At all regular meetings of the Board of Directors, a quorum shall be constituted by the
attendance of one-half (1/2) of both the Executive Committee and the Board of Directors. At

Section 10. Votes
Unless otherwise stated herein, the affirmative votes of not less than a majority of the

Section 11. Proxies
At any meeting of the Board of Directors, a member entitled to vote may vote by proxy

duly authorized attorney-in-fact. A Board member holding the proxy vote may cast the proxy
vote in addition to his/her own vote. A Board member is not allowed to have more than two
(2) proxies at a time.
ARTICLE IV. GENERAL MEMBERSHIP
Section 1. Membership Definitions
A “general member” shall be any person who has shown an active interest in SMGSL by their
participation in present League activities, including parents of girls registered and playing
softball during the current year, coaches, and board members. A person may not be denied
membership based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, or national membership
based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, or national origin.
As used in these By-Laws, a “Board Member” is comprised of at-large members and the
Board of Directors. 
The “Board of Directors” or “Board” is comprised of all of the positions provided in Article
5, Section 1 herein

Section 2. General Members in Meetings
The first thirty (30) minutes of all Board meetings shall be open to all general members with
prior notice to the board. However, if the majority of the Board believes that the subject matter
dictates a closed session for the entirety of the meeting, the League may exclude non-board
members for that meeting only.
Section 3. At-Large Members
Members At-Large shall be any general member that is voted in by a one-half (1/2) majority
of the Board of Directors in which a quorum is present. A one-half (1/2) majority vote of the
current Board of Directors will renew their status every year during the July meeting. Failure
to notify the Secretary of the desire to renew before the vote is called is equivocal to gaining
less than one-half (1/2) majority vote, therefore relinquishing their status voluntarily.
Section 4. Revoking General Membership
General membership is a privilege to those seeking fair competition, good sportsmanship, and fun.
The League may deny membership to, or revoke membership from, any person for any of the
following reasons:
1) Repeated nonincidental violations of League rules
2) Egregious display of disrespect to board members, umpires, coaches, players, or other parents
3) Repeated display of unsportsmanlike conduct, such as excessive argument, repeatedly 
"thrown out" from park, vulgar language
4) Failure to follow lawful directives of Board members
5) Inappropriate or unlawful physical contact of any other person
6) Fighting
7) Harassment, bullying, name calling, gestures of hand, toward or directed to any umpire, 
coach, player, or other parent
8) Filing frivolous lawsuits against the League, board members, or other players, where 
"frivolous" means failure to obtain any relief from the court
9) Public intoxication or use of alcohol or illegal drugs of any kind in the park or parking lots; 
or,
10) Any other basis deemed appropriate by the Executive Committee.
Denial or revocation of membership shall follow the following procedure: Any voting board
member may initiate the process by making a request in writing to the Vice-President of Sports. 
VP of Sports shall then conduct a confidential investigation, as required, and report to the 
Executive Committee its findings. VP of Sports may, but is not required to, make a 
recommendation. The Executive Committee shall then vote whether to deny or revoke 
membership. A two-thirds (2/3rds) affirmative vote is required to deny or revoke membership. 
Where denied or revoked, the Executive Committee shall promptly notify the person in writing 
that their membership has been denied or revoked within 3 business days. Denial or revocation 
may be for one (1) term or permanent. Any further incident thereafter, the person shall be banned 
from the park.
Where membership has been denied or revoked, the person may appeal using the following
procedure: the person must notify the board in writing within 3 business days following receipt.

Section 2. Qualifications to hold offices
A. President. A total of at least three (3) years of active participation (parent of a registered
player, coach, umpire, or other volunteer) at SMGSL. Two (2) of the three (3) years must have
been as a member of the SMGSL Board of Directors. The candidate must also demonstrate a
command of all USA Softball and SMGSL Rules and Regulations.

B. Secretary. The qualified candidate must have served at least a total of three (3) years of
active participation at SMGSL, and one (1) of the two (2) years must be a member of SMGSL
Board of Directors.
C. Treasurer. The qualified candidate must have served at least a total of three (3) years of
active participation at SMGSL. Two (2) of the three (3) years must be a member of the

D. Vice-President of Sports. The qualified candidate must have served for a total of at least

of an SMGSL team or tournament team. The candidate must also demonstrate a command of
all USA Softball and SMGSL Rules and Regulations.
E. Vice-President of Field and Facilities Maintenance. A total of at least three (3) years of
active participation in a girls’ softball League. One (1) of the three (3) years must have been
as a member of the SMGSL League with two (2) years on the SMGSL Board of Directors.
The candidate must have a thorough knowledge of field maintenance and upkeep.
F. Director (s). The qualified candidate must have served at least a total of three (3) years of
active participation at SMGSL. One (1) of the three years must be a member of the SMGSL
Board of Directors.
G. Coordinator (s). The qualified candidate must have served at least one (1) year of active
participation at SMGSL. The candidate must also display a general knowledge of USA
Softball and SMGSL Rules and Regulations.
H.All the above-qualified candidates must be at least twenty-one (21) years of age.
The above qualifications may be waived by a special vote of not less than two-thirds 
(2/3rds) of the members of the Board of Directors.
Section 3. Election Process
The Officers of the League shall be elected in the following manner.
A. Nominations from general members shall be received prior to April 1st of each year for the
Board of Directors to be elected. Nominations must be submitted in writing to the
Nominations Committee and contain information about the position to which a person is
nominated, the name, address, and phone number of the individual so nominated, three (3)
references of the individual so nominated, and the qualifications of the member so
nominated. The Chairperson of the Committee shall not solicit, delete, or add to the ballot.
Only names nominated by the above members shall be placed on the ballot.

B. During the nomination review process, the Nominations Committee may contact the
nominating party and the provided references, as well as the individual nominated, to
determine the nominee’s qualifications and ability to serve. If more than one name is placed
in nomination for a position, the Committee may personally interview the candidate under
consideration.
C. A slate of candidates, including information regarding qualifications and experience, to fill

ballots in person at a time and location established by the League Board. However, all
Members shall receive at least one week’s notice of the election. The affirmative majority
vote of the Members shall be required to fill each remaining position. If no majority is held,
a runoff between the two nominees with the most votes shall be held as soon as possible.
E. For General Nominations, a member can only submit a name for one position within the
SMGSL Board of Directors. If not selected for the position requested, the candidate can
then be appointed to an open position, committee, or member at large.

terms of office with appropriate Board approval and shall be responsible for all League
activities. In addition, the President may appoint one (1) Board Member (current or Ex￾Officio) to serve on the Executive Committee. The President shall be an Ex-officio

President shall be a member of and shall have a vote in the Nominations Committee. In
the absence of the President or in the event of the President's inability or refusal to act, the
board will, by affirmative two-thirds (2/3rds) majority vote, elect a stand-in President.
B. Secretary. The Secretary shall maintain a register of Officers and members of the League,
record the minutes of the League meetings, be responsible for notification of meetings

G. Director of Fundraising. The Director of Fundraising works with local businesses to
establish financial partnerships in the form of sponsorships, donations, or other

Coaches, and Fundraising Events Chairperson(s) to coordinate the Board’s fundraising

H. Director of Safety. The Director of Safety ensures the safety of players and visitors to the

I. Scheduling Coordinator. The Scheduling Coordinator acts as the primary scheduler for all
events that occur at SMGSL fields. He/She creates the practice, game, and tournament
schedule for League and schedules for all other affiliate and non-affiliate events at the
park. He/She also maintains consistent contact with the Head Umpire, Registration
Coordinator, and any other function that requires inputs/outputs into the League calendar.
J. Team Parent Coordinator. The Team Parent Coordinator acts as the primary information
source for league business directly to the Team Parents of all teams. He/She reminds Team
Parents about upcoming events, such as Opening Day, Picture Day, Spirit Nights, and any
other League events and helps Team Parents in a timely manner as required.
K. Registration Coordinator. The Registration Coordinator oversees the registration process
for Fall and Spring leagues. He/She sets up registration for all supported League softball
programs, actively recruits coaches and players, and manages the online registration
process (coordinating, updating, and maintaining online registration). He/She works with
the Treasurer to ensure all payments and refund requests are recorded and received.
He/She is responsible for creating team rosters (coordinate with Scheduling & VP of
Sports) after drafts and finalizes submission through the registration system to families
and head coaches. He/She is responsible for providing the Uniform Coordinator with a
uniform report listing for each team.
L. Uniforms Coordinator. The Uniforms Coordinator is responsible for the ordering of all
uniform elements for the League. He/She is responsible for negotiating with uniform
suppliers, managing cost and delivery requirements, creating uniform designs, and
distributing uniform elements to all teams each season. He/She will also facilitate the
correction of errors to uniforms in a timely manner for players and coaches.

ARTICLE VII. COMMITTEES
Section 1. Committee of Directors
The President, by resolution adopted by a majority of the Directors in office, may designate
and appoint one or more committees, each of which shall consist of two or more Directors,
which committees, to the extent provided in said resolution, shall have and exercise the
authority of the Board of Directors in the management of the League; provided, however, that
no such committee shall have the authority of the Board of Directors in reference to amending,
altering or repealing the By-Laws; electing, appointing or removing any Member of any such
committee or any Director or officer of the League; amending the article of incorporation;
adopting a plan or merger or adopting a plan of consolidation with another League;
authorizing the sale, lease exchange or mortgage of part of or substantially all of the property
and assets of the League; authorizing the voluntary dissolution of the League or revoking
proceedings thereof; adopting a plan for the distribution of assets of the League; or amending,
altering or repealing any resolution of the Board of Directors which by its terms provides that
it shall not be amended, altered or repealed by such committee. The designation and
appointment of any such committee and the delegation thereto of authority shall not operate
to relieve the Board of Directors and any individual Director, of any responsibility imposed
upon it or him/her by law.
Section 2. Standing Committees
The Standing Committees of the League, in addition to the Executive Committee, shall be the
following:
A. Sports Commission. There shall be six (6) members of the Sports Commission who shall
function under the direction of the Vice-President of Sports. The Sports Commissioners
will be appointed by the Vice-President of Sports with final approval by the Board of
Directors.

B. Nominations Committee. It shall be the duty of the Nominations Committee to distribute
nomination forms, collect nomination forms, contact all nominees to determine
availability and interest, and present a slate of nominees to the Board of Directors prior to
May 1st.
C. Protest Committee - The Protest Committee shall consist of the Vice-President of Sports,
the sports commissioner of the division involved, and the Head Umpire. It shall be the
duty of the Vice-President of Sports (Chairperson) to ensure that all members have no
direct involvement with the dispute, including themselves, as well as, selecting Managers
from different age groups other than the one involved in the dispute and invite managers
whose teams are involved in the protest to attend protest hearings.
D. Budget & Finance Committee. It shall be the duty of the Budget & Finance Committee to
prepare a budget on an annual basis and present same to the Executive Committee for
approval. Additional duties include the monitoring of the League's financial position and
presenting a monthly report of said position to the Board. The Treasurer shall serve as the
Chairperson of the Budget and Finance Committee.
E. Disciplinary Committee – It shall be the duty of the Disciplinary Committee to be
involved in all disciplinary actions towards any general member unless deemed a conflict
of interest. The chairperson of this committee shall be the Vice-President of Sports and
shall include a minimum of 2 directors/officers and the commissioner of the involved
division, if applicable. The Disciplinary Committee may solicit the statements of anyone

Section 3. Other Committees
Other committees not having and exercising the authority of the Board of Directors in the
management of the League may be designated by a resolution adopted by a majority of the
Directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present. Except as otherwise provided in
such resolution, members of each such committee shall be members of the League, and the
President of the League shall appoint the members thereof. Any members thereof may be
removed by the person or persons authorized to appoint such member whenever in their
judgment the best interests of the League shall be served by such removal.
Section 4. Term of Office
Each member of a committee shall continue to serve on said committee, such until the next
annual meeting of the members of the League and until his/her successor is appointed, unless
the committee shall be sooner terminated, or unless such member be removed from such
committee, or unless such member shall withdraw or cease to qualify as a member thereof.
Section 5. Rules and Procedures
Each Committee shall adopt its own rules of procedure. However, in no event shall its rules
and procedures conflict with these By-Laws or with the rules adopted by the Board of
Directors.
Section 6. Vacancies
Vacancies in the membership of any committee may be filled by appointments made in the
same manner as provided in the case of the original appointments.
Section 7. Quorum
At no time shall less than a majority of such committee members permitted to vote on any
issue constitute a quorum. Unless otherwise provided herein, the affirmative vote of not less
than a majority of the members present at any meetings, virtual or otherwise, at which there
is a quorum, shall be necessary for the taking of any action by the committee.
Section 8. Minutes
Written minutes shall be kept of all meetings. Actions of each committee shall be reported to
the Executive Committee and/or the Board of Directors. A copy of the minutes will be
submitted to the Executive Committee within 15 (fifteen) days of each meeting.

ARTICLE IX. MISCELLANEOUS
Section 1. Books and Records
The League shall keep correct and complete books and records of accounts and shall also keep
minutes of the proceedings of its members, Board of Directors, and Committees having any
of the authority of the Board of Directors and shall keep a physical or digital copy of the
records on file regarding the members entitled to vote. All books and records of the League
may be inspected by any member, or his agent or attorney for any proper purpose at any
reasonable time.
Section 2. Fiscal Year
The fiscal year of the League shall begin on the first day of October and end on the last day
of September each year.
Section 3. Dissolution
In the event of dissolution of this non-profit corporation, the corporation shall, after paying or
making provisions for the payment of all of the liabilities of the corporation, dispose of all of
the assets of the corporation exclusively for the purpose of the corporation in such manner, or
to such organization or organizations, organized and operated exclusively for charitable,
educational, religious, or scientific purposes and shall at the time qualify as an exempt
organization or organizations under Section 501 (C) (3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954
(or corresponding provision) of any future United States Internal Revenue Law, as the
